1. Information We Collect

When you use PIELAM, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, password, and profile details you provide when signing up.
  • Profile content — photos, bio, tagline, location, and other information you add to your public or business profile.
  • Marketplace activity — products you view, save, rate, share, or upload, and brands/categories you follow.
  • Transaction information — order details, shipping information, and payment confirmation (payment card details are processed by our payment providers and are not stored on our servers).
  • Device & usage data — IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages visited, and interaction logs.
  • Communications — messages you send to us, such as support requests or verification codes (OTP) sent via SMS or email.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account and public/business profile.
  • Operate core features such as product discovery, search, wishlists, ratings, and sharing.
  • Process verification (OTP), sign-in, and password recovery requests.
  • Send transactional notifications, welcome emails, and service updates.
  • Personalize content and improve the discovery/recommendation experience.
  • Monitor, secure, and troubleshoot the platform, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may limit some features of the site.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Other users — information you choose to make public, such as your profile, products, brand pages, or reviews.
  • Service providers — hosting, SMS/email delivery (e.g. OTP providers), analytics, and payment processing partners who help us operate PIELAM.
  • Legal & safety — when required by law, to enforce our policies, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of PIELAM, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal information via your account settings.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (e.g. marketing emails).

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — such as encrypted connections, access controls, and password hashing — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

PIELAM is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and, where appropriate, through additional notice such as email or an in-app notification.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at privacy@pielam.com.
